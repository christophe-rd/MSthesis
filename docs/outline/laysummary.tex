\chapter{Lay Summary}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Climate change lengthens the time during which trees can grow
	\item This should increase their growth; but evidence shows this may not be the case
	\item Here we use young and mature broadleaf trees to understand whether they grow more with longer growing seasons
	\item Young trees grew more, and appear to respond more to end of season temperature than early season
	\item Mature trees did not grow more, which suggest that they are less responsive than their young conterparts
	\item This highlights that climate change may not harm forest regeneration, but could limit future carbon storage as mature trees store most of forest carbon.
\end{enumerate}
