\chapter{Preface}
For my first chapter, I leveraged phenology data that was collected by Dr. D.M. Buonioto and Dr. C.J. Chamberlain in Boston which was part of a project started by Dr. EM Wolkovich. The research project idea was proposed by E.M. Wolkovich and conducted by me with her help throughout. I processed, scanned, measured the tree cross-sections and cores that were collected by Dr. Neil Pederson, Dr. D.M. Buonaiuto, Dr. Deirdre Loughnan and E.M. Wolkovich. I performed the analyses and created the figures with the help of Dr. Victor Van der Meersch and E.M. Wolkovich. Charlotte Capistran Champoux helped with bonifying the figures. I did not use generative artifical intelligence (GenAi) to write this thesis, for any creative or idea generation process. I used GenAi for performing simple repetitive coding tasks, helping with the figure coding, data cleaning and improving coding workflow efficiency. This work will be submitted for publication soon. E.M. Wolkovich and I wrote the manuscript and the following co-authors reviewed and edited it: Victor Van der Meersch, D.M. Buonaiuto, C.J. Chamberlain, Deirdre Loughnan, Neil Pederson.

My second chapter partially consists of leveraging phenology data that was collected by the Tree Spoters, which is citizen science program that E.M. Wolkovich started with C.J. Chamberlain in 2015. The project idea was also proposed by E.M. Wolkovich and conducted by me. I collected the tree cores with Xiaomao Wang's help, and I processed and measured them. I also performed the analyses with Dr. Victor Van der Meersch and E.M. Wolkovich. I used generative artifical intelligence the same way I did for Chapter 1. This work will also be submitted for publication. E.M. Wolkovich and I also wrote the manuscript.
