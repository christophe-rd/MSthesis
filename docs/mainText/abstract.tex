\chapter{Abstract}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Shifted phenology with ACC extends GS; expected growth increase
\item Recent studies failed to find this relationship; with drought and competition restraining growth 
\item Objective: understand if trees grow more with longer GS where drought and competition were limited
\item Leveraging 9 years of phenology from 2 studies, both in an arboretum and by relating it to tree growth
\item Juveniles grew more; adults did not change their growth with longer GS
\item Our results reveal a more complex growth response to climate change than currently assumed
\item Not massive responses and when they do, it's more for juveniles than mature: good for forest regeneration, potentially bad for forest carbon storage.
\end{enumerate}
